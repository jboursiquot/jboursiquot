Works across AI systems, cloud architecture, distributed systems, and reliability engineering. Also a conference keynote speaker and instructor, with courses on O'Reilly, LinkedIn Learning, and Packt.

## Work history

Most recent first, with length of each role. Some roles overlapped.

### Human Agency: Director of Technology (3 mos)

- Restarted a stalled SOC 2 readiness program, delivering automated security controls across all active company systems, vendor tracking, and audit evidence collection.
- Built a commercial controls program ahead of the product's beta launch, including per-customer cost and margin tracking, usage attribution, vendor cost monitoring, and AI-assisted invoice processing workflows.
- Fixed inefficiencies and set up guardrails in a codebase written mostly by non-engineers with AI tools.

### Chick-fil-A (via Stable Kernel): Principal Software Engineer and Program Lead (1 yr 1 mo)

- Lead architecture and delivery for Chick-fil-A's Loyalty Platform initiatives, working with product, engineering, and business teams.
- Cut cache infrastructure costs by 39% and improved latency for high-traffic promotion workloads.
- Traced DynamoDB throttling to thundering-herd cache expiration and designed a phased fix using TTL jitter, request deduplication, and distributed coordination.
- Designed location-scoped cache invalidation and event propagation that reduced promotion-data staleness from minutes to near real time.
- Hire engineers at Stable Kernel, document failure modes, and build Datadog diagnostics for release performance.
- Technologies: DynamoDB, caching, event propagation, Datadog

### Skilltype: Co-founder and CTO (fractional) (6 yrs 11 mos)

- Own product direction and engineering for Skilltype's library talent-management software, from initial concept through launch.
- Build across backend services and React front ends using Go, Java, JavaScript, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, DynamoDB, and AWS; rebuilt the web front end with React, TypeScript, and Vite.
- Build production AI features with Bedrock, Anthropic, and OpenAI, including RAG and tool-calling for customer workflows, and design APIs and MCP integrations for customers.
- Set testing, CI/CD, observability, and secure delivery standards, supporting GDPR-aware architecture, SOC 2-aligned practices, and TX-RAMP compliance.
- Hire and mentor engineers, review designs and code, and work with executive and product teams on what to build.
- Technologies: Go, Java, TypeScript, React, Vite, PostgreSQL, DynamoDB, AWS, Bedrock

### Salesforce: Platform Observability Engineer (1 yr 11 mos)

- Wrote shared Go instrumentation libraries to help service teams adopt OpenTelemetry, and standardized instrumentation patterns across services.
- Led the move from legacy monitoring to Honeycomb and distributed tracing, and helped service owners diagnose production behavior.
- Mentored engineers in Go, observability, and distributed systems.
- Technologies: Go, OpenTelemetry, Honeycomb

### Heroku: Site Reliability Engineer (2 yrs)

- Built tooling and improved incident-response, deployment, and monitoring workflows for Heroku services.
- Served as incident commander during major production incidents, coordinating mitigation and communication.
- Helped Salesforce engineering teams adopt Heroku, and mentored engineers in Go, cloud infrastructure, and distributed systems.

### The Achievement Network: Principal Cloud Engineer (2 yrs 10 mos)

- Led backend engineering and AWS modernization for an education SaaS product.
- Built CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation on AWS, and designed backend services and APIs.
- Guided engineering teams on AWS architecture and implementation.
- Technologies: Go, Java, PostgreSQL, Lambda, SQS, DynamoDB

### InfluxData: Senior Software Engineer (4 mos)

- Worked on distributed time-series database infrastructure and enterprise SaaS services in Go.
- Technologies: Go

### One Door: Software Architect and Principal Software Engineer

- Built retail-merchandising SaaS applications, APIs, and messaging.

## Bios

Either bio may be quoted as-is.

### General

Johnny Boursiquot is a software engineer with over two decades of experience building and running production systems, on startup founding teams and inside large technology companies. He is co-founder and Chief Technology Officer of Skilltype, where he builds LLM-powered products, and he trains and advises engineering teams through his company, Idiomat. Earlier he helped establish Site Reliability Engineering practices at Heroku and contributed to O'Reilly's 97 Things Every SRE Should Know. His courses on Go, cloud, and AI engineering are available on O'Reilly, LinkedIn Learning, and Packt.

### Go community

Johnny Boursiquot is a multi-disciplined software engineer with a love for teaching and community-building. He founded the Baltimore Go User Group and has organized both the Boston Ruby and Boston Go user groups. He is a GopherCon program chair, delivered GopherCon closing keynotes in 2019 and 2021, and teaches workshops at GopherCon events in the US, Europe, and Singapore. He hosted the Go Time podcast from 2017 until its final episode in December 2024. He is co-founder and Chief Technology Officer of Skilltype.
