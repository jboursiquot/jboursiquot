About

I'm a software engineer with over two decades of experience building and running production systems. These days I focus on AI engineering, cloud architecture, and distributed systems. Much of my recent work is getting LLM-powered and agentic systems ready for production, which means explicit control flow, guardrails, and the same observability and testing any other service gets.

I've been on several startup founding teams, including co-founding Skilltype, a cloud-based SaaS platform for workforce development, where I'm CTO. Along the way I was a Program Lead at Chick-fil-A and Director of Technology at Human Agency, and I helped establish site reliability engineering practices at Salesforce/Heroku. Knowing how systems behave once real users arrive shapes most of the engineering decisions I make.

I also speak and teach: conference keynotes, courses on O'Reilly and LinkedIn Learning, and years as a host of the Go Time podcast.
